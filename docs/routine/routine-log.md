# Routine log

One entry per morning, newest at the bottom. Nothing here is edited
afterwards. At the end of the two weeks, the review at the bottom turns
these entries into the status format and the tool list for slices 5
and 7 of `docs/PLAN-portfolio.md`.

Entry format:

```
## YYYY-MM-DD · start HH:MM · end HH:MM

Read: which briefs, `/jobs`, `aigentic status`, anything else.
Looked up: what I had to open beyond the briefs, and why the brief
  did not answer it.
Decided: per project, the job(s) started, or "nothing, because …".
Started: job, project, read-only or writing.
Collided: two jobs or a job and me in one tree; or "none".
Waiting on me: asks, reviews, merges I owed a project.
Questions I asked every project: the same ones as yesterday?
Friction: what took longest, what I did by hand that a tool should do.
```

## Review (fill in after two weeks)

- **The status line a project needs:** the fields I read every day,
  in the order I read them.
- **The questions I asked every day:** each one either becomes an
  orchestrator tool or shows that a tool is missing.
- **How often jobs collided:** sets how urgent slice 3 (worktrees) is.
- **The busiest number of jobs at once:** sets `max_jobs`.
- **Who this is for:** the answer to `docs/FORK.md`'s first open item.
- **Was the routine the same most days?** If yes, slice 8 is in play.
